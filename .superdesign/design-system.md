# PHMS Design System

PHMS is a multi-tenant pharmacy management platform for busy pharmacy, POS, inventory, finance, and laboratory workflows. Interfaces must be operational, readable, low-friction, and suitable for staff with mixed technical confidence.

## Brand

Use the tenant-configurable primary and accent colors. Default primary is deep pharmacy green `#0d2926` and accent is fresh lime `#b8f39a`. Use Inter/system sans-serif only. Keep the existing PHMS logo treatment and real tenant logo. Avoid gradients, decorative fonts, purple/neon colors, and ornamental visual noise.

## Layout and components

Keep the 18rem branded sidebar, sticky white header, 1600px content width, 16px card radius, subtle slate borders, and shallow shadows. Controls use 12px radius and strong labels. Use emerald for completion/paid, amber for pending/partial, rose for danger/unpaid.

## Clinical print documents

Browser-print documents must look professional on A4 and remain legible in black and white. Use a branded but restrained header, structured patient/visit metadata, clean bordered tables, right-aligned monetary columns, compact totals, payment-status badge, clinician/lab signature lines, and footer contact text. Lab invoice and result/prescription report are separate printable views. Never present PDF download as the primary print action.

## Accessibility

Minimum 14px UI body text, clear focus states, sufficient contrast, visible labels, semantic tables, descriptive button labels, and no status conveyed only by color.
